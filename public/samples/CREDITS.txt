Instrument samples
==================

The electric guitar and electric bass samples in this folder are derived from
the Karoryfer Samples free libraries, as packaged by the tonejs-instruments
project.

  - Source library:  Karoryfer Samples  (https://www.karoryfer.com/)
  - Packaging:       tonejs-instruments  (https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments)
  - License:         CC0 / public domain upstream; CC-BY 3.0 as packaged.

Attribution is provided here to satisfy CC-BY 3.0. No warranty. If you
redistribute Quick Tab Maker, keep this file alongside the samples.

Only a sparse subset of pitches is bundled; Tone.Sampler pitch-shifts to fill
the gaps.
